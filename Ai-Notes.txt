# Project Status Notes

## Date
2026-09-10

## Current design
This repository is the active PIC16F723A linear-amplifier protection controller. Obsolete prototype source files have been removed so they cannot enter the production build.

## Firmware
- Direct ADC inputs monitor two forward/reflected SWR pairs, temperature, input power, and drain voltage.
- SWR trips, power scales, drain/input thresholds, temperature scale, sequencer timing, and output polarities are user-configurable from the 1602 LCD menu.
- Hardware comparators combine at INPUT_HARD_FAULT for independent overdrive, drain-peak, and overcurrent protection.
- The sequencer runs TX, TX_VCC, and TX_BIAS with two configurable inter-stage delays, defaulting to 20 ms.
- The LCD uses a PCF8574 backpack driven with software I2C on RC3/RC4 by firmware/src/lcd_i2c.c.

## Display
- The default status page shows post-filter forward power as PEP or RMS and a full-width bracketed PEP bar.
- A second status page shows a PEP bar with temperature in degrees C.
- PEP decay is user-configurable and defaults to 500 ms.
